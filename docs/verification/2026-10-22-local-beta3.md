## Open findings

The earlier ADB-started process group disappeared twice. The exact exit cause is
unproven; lack of a panic and simultaneous process loss do not establish an
Android process-reaper cause. Boot-started observation is a separate test.

Native public UDP routing remains broken: mark 0x80000 resolves through
main/default and reaches rule 5250 unreachable, while unmarked traffic resolves
via Android's wlan0 table. Recurring Rebind messages persist. Existing control
traffic uses the configured loopback proxy. Healthy status and local peer pings
therefore do not establish general public UDP reachability or fresh-login success.

mountify remains disabled across reboot, as expected from its persistent disable
marker. Its original anti-bootloop trigger has not been established or repaired.

The Xiaomi is still on beta.1 runtime. It was not rebooted or upgraded to beta.3
during this OnePlus-focused verification.
