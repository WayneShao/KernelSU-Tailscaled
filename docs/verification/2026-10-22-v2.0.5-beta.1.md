## Boundaries

KernelSU refused its ordinary module-install command on the OnePlus because an
unrelated `mountify` metamodule was disabled after its anti-bootloop handling.
The candidate was therefore extracted from the same verified ZIP into this
module's private staged directory, verified with `kst_verify_bundle`, and
activated with the published `apply-staged` transaction. No reboot occurred.

The maintainer devices retained their existing identities. This verifies engine
upgrade, rollback boundaries, connectivity, and health, but not a destructive
fresh-login test. Issue #1's affected ROM should still test the candidate before
the marked-route part is considered resolved across devices.
